# OLA Ride Analytics - Bengaluru

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes OLA ride booking data to identify patterns in
bookings, cancellations, revenue, vehicle performance, and customer
behavior.

## 🎯 Objective

- To analyze the efficiency of ride - sharing operations in Bengaluru, focusing on the relationship between cancellation, vehicle type and customer ratings

## 📂 Dataset Description

- This dataset contains OLA ride booking data from Bengaluru, India, covering information about bookings, vehicle types, cancellations, ride distance, booking       value, payment methods, and customer ratings. The dataset is used to identify key trends and insights in ride operations and customer behavior.
- Total Rows: 103025
- Total Feature : 19 columns
- Focus Area: Intra – city (Bengaluru Only)

## 💼 Business Problem

The objective is to analyze ride booking data and identify key factors
affecting ride demand, cancellations, revenue, and customer satisfaction.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Excel
- SQL
- Power BI

## 📊 Key Analysis

- Ride booking performance
- Cancellation analysis
- Revenue analysis
- Vehicle type performance
- Customer ratings
- Ride distance analysis

## 💡Key Insights

- The platform recorded a high volume of bookings, indicating strong customer demand and active platform usage.
- A large portion of customer cancellations occurred due to drivers not moving toward pickup locations or asking customers to cancel rides.
- Cash and UPI emerged as the dominant payment methods, whereas card-based payments showed comparatively lower usage.
- Customer and driver ratings across all vehicle categories remained stable around 4.0, indicating relatively consistent service quality.
- Around 62% of bookings were successfully completed, while a significant percentage of bookings were cancelled, impacting operational efficiency

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Reduce ride delays and cancellation frequency to improve customer trust, retention, and repeat bookings.
- Continuously track booking trends, cancellation reasons, customer ratings, and payment behavior to support better business decision-making.
- Optimize nearest-driver matching and pickup management to reduce waiting time and improve ride fulfillment.
- Encourage UPI and card payments through cashback offers, discounts, and loyalty rewards to reduce cash dependency.
